🤖 @ChatNuffBot Update - Version 0.5.0

Hey @ChatNuffBot users! This release is all about smarter, sharper summaries. Here's what's new:

✨ New Features & Improvements
- Smarter summary engine: We've upgraded to a newer language model and rebuilt the summarizer prompts from the ground up. /summary and /gist now produce tighter, more focused TL;DRs that group messages by topic.
- More consistent results: Lowered the model's randomness so re-running /summary on the same chat gives you a more predictable, reliable answer.

🐞 Bug Fixes & Minor Improvements
- More robust handling of messages that contain punctuation like semicolons (previously could confuse the summarizer).
- Updated underlying libraries for better stability and security.
- Various under-the-hood improvements.

📢 We Want Your Feedback!
Thanks for being part of the @ChatNuffBot community! If you notice a difference in your summaries (good or bad), or run into any issues, reach out to @Ashcir — your feedback shapes what comes next.
